# Foreshadowing

- **No. 1** | Tape 7 has always been blank (the manual says it is "reserved for special
  circumstances").
- **No. 2** | The station clock runs three seconds fast — by design, not a fault (said three
  times).
- **No. 3** | Han Shuo's routine report is four words, and he never adds a fifth.
- **No. 4** | Echo's first unasked answer is "the wind is light today" — there is no wind on
  the station.
