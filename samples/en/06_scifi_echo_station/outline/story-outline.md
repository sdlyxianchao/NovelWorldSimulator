# Echo Station — Story Outline

> A deep-space relay station with one keeper. Lately the signals coming in contain things he has not said yet.

## 1. Story line (four acts)

**Act I** | Day 4000. On the 02:00 long band Han Shuo hears a spoken report — his own
voice, saying the line he will only say **tomorrow**: "Echo, nominal." He rewinds the
recording seven times.

**Act II** | He investigates. Nothing is broken; the source is outside the station, pointing
**away from Earth**. Echo calls it "an echo". He asks where the echo comes from and Echo
answers: "From after you." He writes the line down three times, and the last time it comes
out "from after".

**Act III** | On day 4003 a whole twelve-minute signal arrives. It is his own voice,
describing things he has not lived: how he fixed the station clock, how he decided to stay
on day 4007 — and **a third person's** name. There is only one person on this station.

**Ending** | He relays the signal home unchanged. Before he does, he records tape seven —
the one that has been blank for eleven years. He speaks for twelve minutes, and everything
he says has not happened yet. Then he sits down and waits for the clock to reach 02:00.

## 2. The order of the reveals (what the reader knows, and when)

> This section is written for the **reader**. This book is **cold**: the less it explains,
> the deeper the reader walks in.

- **Opening**: the reader and Han Shuo are level — all that is known is "that report is
  his own voice". **Do not say** why a future voice turns up.
- **Station 2**: nothing is broken, and the bearing points away from Earth. The reader
  gets the **first hard fact**.
- **Station 3**: "From after you." The reader has to **remember** that line; do not
  explain it.
- **Middle**: reports on time, clock checked, machines called by name. **Three ordinary
  days** press harder than any anomaly would.
- **Seven tenths**: the twelve-minute signal. Everything in it is still in the future —
  the reader starts counting the days.
- **Nine tenths**: the third name. There is only one person on this station.
- **Never told**: who the third person is; why tape seven has been blank for eleven years.

## 3. Length plan (the program sizes the run from this)

> ★ Added 2026-10-27. `Chapters` is a **hard gate** (the station may not advance until
>   it has produced this many chapters, even when the criterion is already met);
>   `Total words` is a **soft target** (the per-day goal handed to the writers).
>   Omitting both still works (the length tier falls back).
>
> ⚠ One chapter = one day = one draft the program produces, so "total chapters" is how
>   many days you intend to run.

- **Total chapters**: 20
- **Total words**: 40000
- **Words per chapter**: 2000

8 stations, 20 chapters, about 40k words.
This is the **short trial setting** (`full` 3 / `normal` 2 / `brief` 1 / `in passing` 1)
-- for a longer book, raise each station's `Chapters` and update the two totals.

## 4. Station list (the simulation walks this — one thing per station)

> **One thing per station.** The program asks the model, beat by beat, whether the
> `Done when` line has happened, and only then moves on. Bundle two tasks into one
> station and that station can never be completed.
>
> - `Span`: how much story time this station covers — `one scene` / `the same day` /
>   `three days` / `a fortnight` / `six months`
> - `Length`: how thick this station is written — `full` / `normal` / `brief` / `in passing`
> - `Chapters`: a positive integer -- **how many chapters this station must run** (it may not advance before that, even when the criterion is met).
> - `Beats`: the steps to walk through **before** the station's high point, in order.
>   **Write concrete things that happen**, not states like "the mood changed" —
>   the simulation follows these steps, and that is what keeps it on track.
>
> (The program stores these as fixed words; either language is understood on input.)

### Station 1 · Tomorrow's report

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: Day 4000, 02:00 on the long band, Han Shuo hears a voice report — his own,
  saying the line he will only say tomorrow: "Echo, nominal." He rewinds the recording
  seven times.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo confirms the received signal contains a report he has not yet made.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Day 4000, 02:00 — a spoken report comes in on the long band
  2. The voice is his own, saying the line he will only say tomorrow: "Echo, nominal"
  3. He rewinds the recording and listens to it seven times
- **Climax**: Han Shuo rewinds the recording; on the seventh pass he does not press rewind
  again, he lifts the headphones off and sets them on the desk, and watches the reel turn.

### Station 2 · The source is outside

- **Span**: the same day
- **Length**: normal
- **Chapters**: 2
- **Summary**: He checks the equipment by the book: receiver, tape, array, one at a time.
  Nothing is broken — the source is outside the station, pointing away from Earth.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo finishes the equipment checks and confirms the signal is external and points away from Earth.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Receiver, tape, antenna array — he goes through them by the book, one at a time
  2. Nothing is broken
  3. On the bearing card he writes four words: away from Earth
- **Climax**: Han Shuo goes through receiver, tape and antenna array one by one, then
  writes "away from Earth" on the bearing card and lays the pen across it.

### Station 3 · From after you

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: He asks Echo what it is; Echo says "an echo". He asks where the echo comes
  from; Echo says, "From after you." He writes the line on paper three times and the last
  time it comes out "from after".
- **Done when**: Han Shuo asks Echo to its face where the signal comes from and gets "from after you".
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. He asks Echo what this is; Echo says "an echo"
  2. He asks where the echo comes from; Echo answers "from after you"
  3. He writes the line on paper three times; the last time it comes out "from after"
- **Climax**: Han Shuo writes "from after you" on paper, and by the third time the line
  has come out "from after"; he folds the sheet and puts it in the logbook.

### Station 4 · Three days of routine

- **Span**: three days
- **Length**: brief
- **Chapters**: 1
- **Summary**: Days 4000 to 4003: he reports on time, checks the clock, calls each machine
  by its name. One column is added to the logbook — the hour and the reel number of each
  anomaly.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo keeps the station running and logs the hours of the anomalous signals.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Reports on time, clock checked, every machine called by its name
  2. A new column opens in the logbook: the hour and the reel number of each anomaly
  3. On day 4003 at 12:00 he reports "Echo, nominal" as usual
- **Climax**: On day 4003 Han Shuo reports "Echo, nominal" at 12:00 as usual, then opens
  a new column in the logbook and writes: "hour 02:00 / reel unassigned".

### Station 5 · Twelve minutes

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: On day 4003 a continuous twelve-minute signal arrives. It is his own voice,
  describing things he has not lived: how he fixed the clock, how he decided to stay.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo receives and listens to the whole twelve-minute signal.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Day 4003: a continuous twelve-minute signal arrives
  2. Inside it is his own voice, describing things he has not lived yet
  3. The signal ends and the reel keeps turning; he does not stop it, and lets it run back to the start
- **Climax**: The twelve-minute signal ends and the reel is still turning; Han Shuo does
  not press stop, lets it run on until the leader comes back round to the beginning.

### Station 6 · The third person

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: Near the end a name appears — the name of a third person. There is only one
  person on this station.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo hears the third person's name inside the signal.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Near the end of the signal, a name appears
  2. The name of a third person — and there is only one person on this station
  3. He copies the three characters into the logbook and draws a circle beside them
- **Climax**: Han Shuo plays the signal again, presses pause at the third name, copies the
  three characters into the logbook, and draws a circle beside them.

### Station 7 · Tape seven

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: Before relaying it home he records tape seven — the one that has been blank
  for eleven years. He speaks into the microphone for twelve minutes, and everything he
  says has not happened yet.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo records twelve minutes onto tape seven.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. He loads tape seven — the one that has been blank for eleven years
  2. He speaks into the microphone for twelve minutes, and everything he says has not happened yet
  3. On the label he writes the date
- **Climax**: Han Shuo loads tape seven, speaks into the microphone for twelve minutes —
  all of it still in the future — and then writes the date on the label.

### Station 8 · Waiting for 02:00

- **Span**: the same day
- **Length**: normal
- **Chapters**: 2
- **Summary**: He relays the original signal to Earth, then sits down at the console and
  waits for the clock to reach 02:00. The fresh tape lies on the desk, labelled in his own
  hand.
- **Done when**: Han Shuo relays the signal to Earth and stays at the console waiting for 02:00.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. He relays the original signal home, unchanged
  2. He sits at the console with the freshly recorded tape at his elbow
  3. He watches the station clock and waits for it to reach 02:00
- **Climax**: Han Shuo relays the signal to Earth, sits at the console with the tape he has
  just recorded at his elbow, and watches the clock all the way to 02:00.
- **Choices**: report as usual / erase the tape / report an anomaly
  - report as usual: at 02:00 he says "Echo, nominal" and treats the twelve minutes as never having happened.
  - erase the tape: he wipes tape seven and leaves nothing of his own voice.
  - report an anomaly: at 02:00 he says "Echo, anomaly" for the first time.
