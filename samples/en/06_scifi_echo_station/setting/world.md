# World

> Echo Station — Science Fiction

Relay station **Echo**, parked in a stretch of deep space with nothing much to look at.
Its job is simple: amplify signals from Earth and pass them outward; receive signals from
further out and pass them home.

One person at a time. The rotation cycle is eleven years — not a regulation, just what
**works**: eleven years is about as long as one person can hold together, and about as long
as one set of equipment survives.

The keeper's day is always the same:
- 06:00 — inspection walk (380 steps, one circuit)
- 12:00 — record the routine report on Tape 7
- 18:00 — recalibrate the station clock (it runs **three seconds fast**; this is by design,
  not a fault)
- 02:00 — listen on longwave

The station's system is called Echo. Synthetic female voice. It answers your questions, and
sometimes, when you have not asked one, it says something of its own.
