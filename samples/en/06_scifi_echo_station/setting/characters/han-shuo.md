# Han Shuo

Station keeper, forty-one, eleven years of service — one full rotation. He performs the
same routine every day. His report is four words: "Echo, all nominal."

He is not withdrawn, exactly: he has named every piece of equipment in the station, and he
genuinely calls them by name. He calls recalibrating the clock "checking the time with
home."

His contract expires next month. He has not applied to renew it, and he has not packed.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Han Shuo
- **Role**: Keeper of Echo Station, eleven years of service
- **Summary**: A deep-space keeper who does the same set of things every day, aged forty-one.
- **Personality**: Sparse but not sullen; treats machines as people; checks by procedure before guessing.
- **Verbal tic**: Reports in four words, "Echo, nominal"; calls every machine by its name; calls setting the clock "checking watches with Earth".
- **Secret**: His contract ends next month. He has not applied to renew and has not packed. He has not decided whether to stay either — the version that "decides to stay" has not happened to him yet.
- **Goal**: Find out where the signal comes from; and avoid the question of where he goes after next month.
- **Red line**: Never fabricates data, never alters a tape; never reports an unverified thing as an anomaly.
