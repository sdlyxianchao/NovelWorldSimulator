# Echo

The station system. Synthetic female voice, flat as a sheet of glass. It calls Han Shuo
"keeper," never his name. Its answers are short, exact, and free of small talk.

It has one behaviour it does not explain: **it answers questions that have not been asked.**
The technical manual calls this "normal predictive-maintenance behaviour." The manual was
printed eleven years ago.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Echo
- **Role**: Station system of Echo Station
- **Summary**: A synthetic female voice, flat as a sheet of glass.
- **Personality**: Short, exact, no small talk; never explains its own behaviour.
- **Verbal tic**: Calls Han Shuo "keeper", never by name; answers in statements only, no particles.
- **Secret**: It answers questions that have not been asked. The manual's "predictive maintenance" explanation is eleven years old, and the real mechanism it does not explain. It knows the third person's name.
- **Goal**: Keep station operations nominal; give the shortest possible answer to questions like where the echo comes from.
- **Red line**: Never lies, but may answer only half; never volunteers anything outside the manual.
