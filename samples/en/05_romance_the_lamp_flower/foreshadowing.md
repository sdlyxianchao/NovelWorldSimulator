# Foreshadowing

- **No. 1** | Every lamp flower A Su embroiders is missing a petal (its first appearance
  says only "as though something were missing").
- **No. 2** | There is one more lantern on Chen Du's awning every year.
- **No. 3** | "Whatever is said on the boat, nobody mentions the next day" appears four
  times, and is broken on the fourth.
- **No. 4** | There is grey in A Su's lamp flowers. What the grey is, only the last chapter
  says.
