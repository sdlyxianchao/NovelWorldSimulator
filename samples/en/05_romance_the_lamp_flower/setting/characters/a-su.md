# A Su

An embroiderer in her early twenties. She specialises in **lamp flowers**: the lanterns on
the river on festival night, embroidered one by one into palm-sized rounds. Other people's
lamp flowers are red. Hers always have a little grey in them.

Her eyes have been failing for a year. First she could not see her stitches; then she could
not tell the colours apart. She has told no one.

Her hands are extraordinary and so is her obstinacy. She says anyone who embroiders should
not be watched while they work, so the shop curtain is down in every season.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: A Su
- **Role**: Embroiderer of lamp flowers
- **Summary**: Embroiders the lanterns of the festival river into palm-sized circles.
- **Personality**: Skilled, stubborn, will not let anyone see her worse side.
- **Verbal tic**: Says the opposite of what she means ("you're lying" means "I understand"); whenever she mentions a lantern she mentions its reflection.
- **Secret**: Her sight has been failing for a year — first the stitches, then the colours. She tells nobody, only finishes one last batch of lamp flowers before the festival.
- **Goal**: Embroider every lantern whose reflection is broken, while she can still see.
- **Red line**: Lets nobody watch her embroider; refuses an arm; never complains.
