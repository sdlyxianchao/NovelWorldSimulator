# World

> The Lamp Flower — Romance

A river town in Jiangnan, where the waterways run crosswise and you cross by ferry. On the
night of the Lantern Festival the river carries lanterns, the landing is packed, and the
ferryman rows several dozen crossings.

There is a local saying: the **last crossing** of the festival **takes no strangers**.
The ferryman chooses. Whoever he chooses sits in the boat all night — downriver to the
head of the water and back, coming ashore at dawn.

On that crossing no one sings, no one proposes, no one passes anything from hand to hand.
There is exactly one rule: **whatever is said on the boat, nobody mentions the next day.**

Which is why the most famous thing about this river is that nobody knows what happens on
the last crossing. All anyone knows is that every year after the festival, the landing has
a few more lanterns on it.
