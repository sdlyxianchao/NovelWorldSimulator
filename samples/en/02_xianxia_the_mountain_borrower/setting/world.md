# World

> The Mountain Borrower — Xianxia / Cultivation

The cultivation world does not run on plunder. It runs on **borrowing**. A mountain can
be borrowed; a river can be borrowed; a person's last breath can be borrowed. There is
only one rule: **what you borrow must be returned. If you cannot return it, it goes on
the ledger.**

Where is the ledger kept? **On the borrower.** Every loan leaves a mark — a scar that
looks old and burned, painless, but countable. A wandering cultivator with too many marks
is driven off wherever he goes. Who knows whom he will borrow from next.

**Borrowing a mountain** is the heaviest work in the trade. When a mountain's *form* is
taken, the mountain does not collapse. Instead the people inside it **lose where they came
from**: they cannot leave, and they cannot say where they belong. So a mountain loan
requires a contract, and the contract must state when it will be returned and in what
condition.

Of the wandering cultivators, only one still does this work. He says the job is not hard.
The hard part is **the returning**.
