# A Ruo

The mountain's keeper. Looks sixteen or seventeen, wears the same blue cotton clothes
in every season. Speaks little; ask three questions and you get one answer, and the
answer is often to a question you did not ask.

She guards the mountain beyond reason. Snap a single twig on it and she will hold it
against you for three years.

She does not know she is **the form this mountain took**. She thinks "keeper of the
mountain" is a post, and that her replacement has not arrived yet, and that therefore
she is not allowed to leave.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: A Ruo
- **Role**: Keeper of Guanyun Mountain
- **Summary**: A girl of sixteen or seventeen in the same blue cotton every season; guards the mountain beyond reason.
- **Personality**: Speaks little, holds to the letter, values the mountain above people.
- **Verbal tic**: Ask three questions and get one answer, often to a question you did not ask; calls the mountain "it", never "this mountain".
- **Secret**: She is the form this mountain took — and does not know it. She thinks "keeper" is a post and that a replacement is still to come.
- **Goal**: Keep the mountain; wait for whoever takes over from her.
- **Red line**: Snap a twig on the mountain and she holds it against you for three years. Never lets the mountain take any harm.
