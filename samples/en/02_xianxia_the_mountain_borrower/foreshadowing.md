# Foreshadowing

- **No. 1** | Lu Xingzhou always counts his marks from left hand to right, and always
  ends one over.
- **No. 2** | Half a line of small writing is struck out on the contract (its first
  appearance is only described as "a scratch").
- **No. 3** | A Ruo answers the wrong question — the answers are usually what **the
  mountain** thinks, not what she thinks.
- **No. 4** | The seal at the bottom of the contract belongs to the Cloudbrook Sect's
  **founder**, not its current master.
