# Shen Yan

Doctor of the Clock Room, around thirty. Speaks little; his hands are extraordinarily
steady — he can set a float to within half a hair while carrying on a conversation.

He has a skill nobody else values: **reading people out of the speed of water.**
If the water ran cloudy at a certain hour, someone dipped into it. If one stretch of the
scale is polished bright, someone was watching that stretch over and over. He calls this
"bookkeeping by leak."

He looks cold and is secretly protective: if someone in the Room gets into trouble, he
takes it on himself first and asks questions privately afterward. People say he keeps
the rules. Only he knows he keeps **a different set**.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Shen Yan
- **Role**: Doctor of the Clock Room, Bureau of Astronomy
- **Summary**: A clerk who tends the water clocks and solves crimes by how fast the water ran.
- **Personality**: Speaks little, steady hands, secretly protective. Looks like the most
  rule-abiding man in the Room; in fact he keeps a different set of rules.
- **Verbal tic**: Trade jargon ("bookkeeping by leak", "the water ran cloudy here"); always
  gives the count with the hour — never "early", always "a quarter-hour early".
- **Secret**: the page missing from the ledger ten years ago is his own first signed clock
  check; his senior took the blame for him. He has always known and never dared look.
- **Goal**: put the seven clock changes on one sheet and find who is moving the city's clocks.
- **Red line**: no flashes of insight, no luck — every clue must have been shown to the
  reader first. Never exposes a colleague in public; he asks in private.
