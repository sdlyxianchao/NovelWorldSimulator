# Liu Niang

A widow who sells sesame flatbread in the West Market, in her thirties, with a voice loud
enough to drown a street. Bread by day, messages by night — not a gangster, but nobody in
Chang'an knows better which house will leave its lamps unlit tonight.

Her memory is frightening: who bought how many cakes, who overpaid by three coins,
who came in with a bruise. She retells it verbatim.

Her attitude toward Shen Yan is odd. At first she avoided him; later she started leaving
bread at the Bureau gate. She says it is on her way. She has been "on her way" forty times
a year.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Liu Niang
- **Role**: Widow selling sesame flatbread in the West Market; carries messages by night
- **Summary**: Sells bread by day and messages by night — she knows which house leaves its lamps unlit.
- **Personality**: Loud, frightening memory, never admits anything.
- **Verbal tic**: quotes the price before anything else ("three coins, no more"); retells
  other people's words verbatim, imitating the tone.
- **Secret**: one of the messages she carried was for Shen Yan's senior. She did not know
  it would get someone killed — she only knew she was paid, which is why she avoids him.
- **Goal**: keep her network of eyes and ears on this street; and make Shen Yan owe her once.
- **Red line**: never touches anyone from the Bureau; never carries a message that sends a
  man to his death — though she cannot tell which ones those are.
