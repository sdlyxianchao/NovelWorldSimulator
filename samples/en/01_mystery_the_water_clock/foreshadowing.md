# Foreshadowing

- **No. 1** | Shen Yan's left little finger is severed. The reason appears twice in the
  whole book; the second time is in the final chapter.
- **No. 2** | Every case ends with the line "Water does not lie. People do." — and each
  time it is a different person saying it.
- **No. 3** | The Room's ledger is missing the page for ten years ago (a torn paper edge
  is visible beside the gap).
- **No. 4** | The extra coins Liu Niang always pays: exactly three, never two, never four.
