# Foreshadowing

- **No. 1** | The book in the till that is never sold (the text never says what it is).
- **No. 2** | Lin Xiaoman overpays by one coin every time and leaves it on the counter.
- **No. 3** | Half the sign is burnt out — "NIGHT B OK." Is the missing word "BOOK" or
  "READ"?
- **No. 4** | The books Zhou Chi can hear grow brighter; only his own grows darker.
