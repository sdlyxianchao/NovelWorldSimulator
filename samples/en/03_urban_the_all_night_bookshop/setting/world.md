# World

> The All-Night Bookshop — Urban / Contemporary

Present day. On the corner of an old street in the south of the city there is a
24-hour bookshop. Half the sign is burnt out; from a distance it reads like
"NIGHT B OK."

The shop has three unwritten rules:
1. **Never push a book.** Ask which one is good and the owner will only say,
   "That one hasn't been read yet."
2. **Between three and four in the morning, do not ask a customer about their job.**
3. **The book in the till is not for sale.**

The owner has a condition: he can **hear which books want to be read**. Books that have
been read glow a little. Books nobody has touched for a long time go dim — not as a
figure of speech; he watches them dim. Which is why his shop only stocks what nobody
else stocks: remainders, dead titles, out-of-print things.

He also has one book he cannot hear: **his own.**
