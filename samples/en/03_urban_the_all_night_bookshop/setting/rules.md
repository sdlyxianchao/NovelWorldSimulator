# Top-level Rules

> This book's hard rules. When they conflict with the plot or a character's wishes,
> **these win.**

## World laws
- Zhou Chi's ability to **hear which books want to be read** is the *only* supernatural
  element in the book. Nothing else in it may be supernatural.
- It tells him only which book is bright and which is dim: he **cannot** read minds,
  foresee events, or decide anything for anyone.
- Lin Xiaoman comes every Wednesday around three, stays forty minutes, buys the dimmest
  book — that pattern may not break early, unless Zhou Chi gives up his own book first.

## How it is written
- **Never explain** the "sound": it is real, but the book gives no mechanism and nobody
  in it ever names it.
- No confessions and no explanations between the two of them; the relationship only moves
  through a book handed over, the extra coin, the thermos.
- End each chapter on one unexplained object (in this book: the rice porridge, the
  extra coin).
