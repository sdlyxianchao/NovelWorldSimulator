# Zhou Chi

Owner of the shop, mid-thirties, a former publishing editor. No website, no advertising,
no discounts. He buys stock by how bright it is.

He hands over books the way a doctor writes a prescription: he does not ask what you
want, he asks what time you go to sleep.

He seems easygoing. There is one thing he cannot get past — the day he quit, the book he
was responsible for was the one he turned into "a book nobody would ever read."
Since then he cannot hear his own.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Zhou Chi
- **Role**: Owner of a 24-hour bookshop; former publishing editor
- **Summary**: No website, no advertising, no discounts; buys stock by how bright it is.
- **Personality**: Seems easygoing, has one thing he cannot get past; his care for people lives in his gestures.
- **Verbal tic**: Hands over books like a doctor writing a prescription — never "what do you want", always "what time do you go to sleep".
- **Secret**: The book in the till that he cannot hear is one he wrote — the day he quit he turned it into "a book nobody would ever read". He cannot hear it because he dares not.
- **Goal**: Work out, before the shop closes, why that book neither sounds nor dims.
- **Red line**: Never explains why he left publishing; never recommends a book, only hands one over.
