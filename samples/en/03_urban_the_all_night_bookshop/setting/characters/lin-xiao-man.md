# Lin Xiaoman

Night-shift nurse. Comes in around three in the morning every Wednesday, stays forty
minutes, buys one book, leaves. Never says what shift she works. Never asks about the shop.

She buys strangely: she never browses. She takes the **dimmest** book on the shelf.
She always overpays by one coin, leaves it on the corner of the counter, and walks out
before anyone can make change.

In the third year she starts bringing a thermos. It is full of plain rice porridge.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Lin Xiaoman
- **Role**: Night-shift nurse
- **Summary**: Comes in around 03:00 every Wednesday, stays forty minutes, buys one book, leaves.
- **Personality**: Sparse, answers only what is asked, keeps her own order of doing things.
- **Verbal tic**: Never says which shift she works; answers often start "I am not sure — I only know..."
- **Secret**: She has begun to forget things. She returns the books she bought because she is afraid of the day she can no longer read them; she calls it "finished".
- **Goal**: Clear what she owes the shop; confirm that the book waiting for someone is not waiting for her.
- **Red line**: Never mentions her illness; never asks about the shop's private business; never accepts being looked after.
