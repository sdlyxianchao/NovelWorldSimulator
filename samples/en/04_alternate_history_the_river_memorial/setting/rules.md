# Top-level Rules

> This book's hard rules. When they conflict with the plot or a character's wishes,
> **these win.**

## World laws
- Once the River Memorial is sealed, its numbers are fixed: a sealed number may not be
  altered. Change a number and every earlier administration's accounts fall apart — and
  privately altering the River Memorial is punished by execution.
- A one-chi error is copied forward, generation after generation. It can be repaid only in
  the next levee, the next memorial, and repaid with clean reasoning a superior can read
  himself — the law cannot change, but a method can be new.
- The one chi and two cun of levee height is the root of the whole matter. Everything comes
  down to three things: who measured, who signed, who will measure again.
- Flood control rests on no heaven and no god, only on the numbers in the River Memorial.
  A wrong number cannot be pushed off onto the sky or onto a god.

## How it is written
- Short sentences, plain description; write only the memorial, the seal, the rod, the files,
  the levee — no fine scenery, no feeling, no long passages of inner life.
- Each chapter advances one step of the middle; foreshadowing may only be planted, never
  shown down inside a chapter, never paid off.
- No information that a single river-works registrar could not have. Pei Ji can carry a sum
  to the fen; he cannot see into the court.
