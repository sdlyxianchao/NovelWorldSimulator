# World

> The River Memorial — Alternate History

The Zhao dynasty. In the north, a great river floods every three years and catastrophically
every ten. Flood control does not rest on men; it rests on the **River Memorial** — a
document that computes levee height, diversion, flood splitting and materials. It is drawn
up, sealed, and archived.

**Once sealed, it cannot be altered.** Not the words — the numbers. Alter a number and every
previous administration's accounts fall apart, and under Zhao law, privately altering the
River Memorial is punished by **execution**.

Thus the system produced a strange creature: **everyone knows the sums are wrong, and nobody
may correct them.** So generation after generation of river-works clerks learned a craft —
an error must be repaid in the **next** memorial, and repaid so thoroughly, with such clean
reasoning, that a superior can see it for himself.

And this year the flood season came a month early.
