# Cui Ling

County magistrate, early thirties, a degree-holder, a better arithmetician than Pei Ji and
a worse politician.

He sees the whole board: alter the memorial and die, leave it and more people die. He wants
neither, so he spends his days being boiled in the middle.

One odd habit: he **recopies every memorial by hand** before it goes up, saying a scribe's
hand is on record and his should not be. And whenever he recopies, he changes one character.

## Character sheet

- **Name**: Cui Ling
- **Role**: County magistrate; holder of a degree
- **Summary**: A better arithmetician than Pei Ji and a worse politician.
- **Personality**: Sees the whole board, wants neither death, so spends his days being boiled in the middle; mild in manner, ruthless with the brush.
- **Verbal tic**: Speaks in officialese ("the memorial is sealed", "as is customary"); says "I shall recopy it" and explains nothing further.
- **Secret**: Three years ago he already wrote that the method could be new — and never dared submit it. He kept that draft, waiting for someone else to break the matter open first.
- **Goal**: Hold the levee and keep both their lives — the law cannot change, so move the method.
- **Red line**: Never lies, never frames a colleague; never writes a false date on a memorial (but may write it vaguely).
