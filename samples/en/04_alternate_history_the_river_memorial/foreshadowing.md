# Foreshadowing

- **No. 1** | Pei Ji's abacus is missing one bead (the fifteenth rod). He has used it
  unchanged for fifteen years.
- **No. 2** | Cui Ling recopies every memorial by hand and "happens" to change one character.
- **No. 3** | The flood date is revised repeatedly in the narrative (the seventh, the
  twelfth, the nineteenth) — each revision is somebody waiting for something.
- **No. 4** | The name on the fifteen-year-old memorial appears as a surname only, always
  the same one.
