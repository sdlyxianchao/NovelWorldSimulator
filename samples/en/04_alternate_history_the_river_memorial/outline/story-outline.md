# The River Memorial — Story Outline

> An invented dynasty. A junior river-works clerk discovers that the sealed flood plan is one chi short. And altering it is a capital crime.

## 1. Story line (four acts)

**Act I** | The flood season arrives early. Pei Ji re-checks the old memorial and finds that
one stretch of the west levee is short by one chi — not a slip of the brush, but an error
inherited from fifteen years ago and copied forward edition after edition. He computes it
three times with the same result: the river will top the levee by **one chi and two cun**.

**Act II** | He tries the first road: report it. His superior reads it and says only,
"The memorial is sealed." He tries the second: raise money from the local gentry. They will
pay, on condition that a stele go up on the levee — with their names on it, not the
clerks'. Pei Ji refuses.

**Act III** | Eleven days to the flood. Pei Ji does the least Pei-Ji-like thing of his life:
**he alters the archived copy in his own keeping.** One chi becomes two; he adds an
explanatory note, signs it, and dates it fifteen years ago.

**Ending** | The levee holds. Afterward the alteration is traced, cleanly and completely.
As Pei Ji is led away, Cui Ling hands up the file — and inside it is a **new memorial**, in
which Pei Ji's alteration is described as "corrected according to the new reckoning," and
the source of that reckoning is a memorial Cui Ling submitted three years earlier. The law
cannot change. But a method can be new. The following year, the court established one.

## 2. The order of the reveals (what the reader knows, and when)

> This section is written for the **reader**. Saying a thing three beats early or three
> beats late changes the taste of the whole book.

- **Opening**: the reader knows the levee is one chi short — **not who signed the
  edition from fifteen years ago**.
- **Station 2**: "the memorial is sealed" — that is the **rule**, not an obstruction. The
  reader has to feel its weight.
- **Station 3**: the gentry will pay (the reader relaxes), on condition of a stele —
  tight again, immediately.
- **Middle**: eleven days left. The reader starts counting the days for him.
- **Seven tenths**: he alters the copy in his own keeping. First time the reader knows
  what he will choose.
- **Nine tenths**: the new memorial goes into the file. The reader and Pei Ji understand
  at the same moment that Cui Ling has been waiting for this.
- **Never told**: what Cui Ling's memorial of three years ago actually said; who signed
  the edition from fifteen years ago.

## 4. Station list (the simulation walks this — one thing per station)

> **One thing per station.** The program asks the model, beat by beat, whether the
> `Done when` line has happened, and only then moves on. Bundle two tasks into one
> station and that station can never be completed.
>
> - `Span`: how much story time this station covers — `one scene` / `the same day` /
>   `three days` / `a fortnight` / `six months`
> - `Length`: how thick this station is written — `full` / `normal` / `brief` / `in passing`
> - `Chapters`: a positive integer -- **how many chapters this station must run** (it may not advance before that, even when the criterion is met).
> - `Beats`: the steps to walk through **before** the station's high point, in order.
>   **Write concrete things that happen**, not states like "the mood changed" —
>   the simulation follows these steps, and that is what keeps it on track.
>
> (The program stores these as fixed words; either language is understood on input.)

### Station 1 · One chi short

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: The flood season comes early. Re-checking the west levee memorial, Pei Ji
  finds it short by one chi, inherited from fifteen years ago. He computes it three times:
  the river will top it by one chi and two cun.
- **Done when**: Pei Ji calculates that the west levee is one chi short and the river will top it by one chi and two cun.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. The flood season comes early; Pei Ji lays out the west levee memorial and re-checks it
  2. Short by one chi — the error comes from the edition of fifteen years ago, copied forward
  3. He sets out his counting rods three times and gets the same answer: over by one chi and two cun
- **Climax**: Pei Ji lays out his counting rods once more; on the third round he puts the
  last rod across the edge of the desk and does not put it away.

### Station 2 · The memorial is sealed

- **Span**: the same day
- **Length**: normal
- **Chapters**: 2
- **Summary**: He reports it. His superior reads the file and says only, "The memorial is
  sealed" — once sealed it cannot be touched. He has said his piece; the rest is not his
  to decide.
- **Done when**: Pei Ji reports the matter and is turned back with "the memorial is sealed".
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. He presents the file; the superior opens it and reads
  2. The superior says only "the memorial is sealed"
  3. The file is closed and handed back; what was left to say has been said
- **Climax**: The superior closes the file and hands it back, saying only "the memorial
  is sealed"; Pei Ji takes it back and does not open his mouth again.

### Station 3 · The stele condition

- **Span**: three days
- **Length**: brief
- **Chapters**: 1
- **Summary**: He asks the local gentry to pay for raising the levee. They will — on the
  condition that a stele go up with their names on it, not the clerks'. Pei Ji refuses,
  and takes no money.
- **Done when**: Pei Ji raises the money question with the gentry and it falls through over the stele.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. He asks the gentry to pay for raising and reinforcing the levee; the silver arrives by sedan chair
  2. They will pay on condition that a stele goes up with their names on it, not the clerks'
  3. Pei Ji refuses, and takes no money
- **Climax**: The gentry pushes the drafted stele text across; Pei Ji reads it, pushes the
  paper back, leaves the silver on the table untouched, and says not one word.

### Station 4 · Eleven days left

- **Span**: the same day
- **Length**: normal
- **Chapters**: 2
- **Summary**: Eleven days to the flood. Reported and refused; both roads are shut. In the
  river office he crosses the days off one by one, and stops with the brush over his own
  archived copy.
- **Done when**: Pei Ji confirms eleven days remain and that no other road is left.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Reported and refused; both roads are shut
  2. In the river office he crosses the days off one by one
  3. The brush stops over his own archived copy
- **Climax**: Pei Ji crosses the days off the river-office calendar one by one; at the
  eleventh he stops and lays the brush down on his own archived copy.

### Station 5 · Altering the memorial

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: Pei Ji alters the copy in his own keeping: one chi becomes two, an
  explanatory note is added, he signs it, and dates it fifteen years ago. Then he washes
  the brush and dries the paper as if nothing happened.
- **Done when**: Pei Ji changes one chi to two on his own archived copy of the memorial and signs it.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. He lays out his own archived copy, grinds the ink, and smooths the paper flat
  2. One chi becomes two; he adds an explanatory note and signs his own name
  3. He dates it fifteen years ago, washes the brush, and dries the paper
- **Climax**: Pei Ji changes one chi to two, adds a line of explanation, signs his own
  name and dates it fifteen years ago; then he washes the brush and hangs the paper to dry.

### Station 6 · The levee holds

- **Span**: three days
- **Length**: brief
- **Chapters**: 1
- **Summary**: The flood comes. The raised west levee meets the water and it falls back at
  the crest. Below the levee they thank heaven; nobody asks why this stretch is higher
  than the plan.
- **Done when**: The flood recedes without overtopping the west levee.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. The flood comes; that stretch of the west levee stands two chi higher than the plan
  2. The water reaches the crest and falls back
  3. Below the levee they kneel and thank heaven; nobody asks why this stretch is higher
- **Climax**: The flood draws back to the foot of the levee; not one inch of the west
  levee is overtopped. Below it they kneel in a row and thank heaven, and nobody asks why
  this stretch is higher than the plan.

### Station 7 · Traced cleanly

- **Span**: the same day
- **Length**: normal
- **Chapters**: 2
- **Summary**: Afterwards the alteration is traced at once: the hand, the signature, the
  back-dated day, all in his own file. Pei Ji does not argue a single word.
- **Done when**: The alteration is traced to Pei Ji and he is found guilty.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. Afterwards the alteration is traced at once; the altered page is laid out in court
  2. The hand, the signature and the back-dated day all match
  3. Pei Ji does not argue a single word
- **Climax**: The clerk of the court lays the altered page out before the bench; the hand,
  the signature and the date all match. Pei Ji does not argue a word.

### Station 8 · The new memorial

- **Span**: one scene
- **Length**: full
- **Chapters**: 3
- **Summary**: At the hearing Cui Ling hands up the file — inside it a new memorial: Pei
  Ji's change called "corrected by the new reckoning", sourced from a memorial he wrote
  three years ago. Law cannot change; method can be new.
- **Done when**: Cui Ling submits the new memorial at the hearing, turning the alteration into a correction by new reckoning.
- **Beats** (3 units):
  1. At the hearing Cui Ling hands up the file
  2. Inside it is a new memorial: Pei Ji's change is called "corrected by the new reckoning"
  3. The source is a memorial he wrote three years ago — law cannot change, method can be new
- **Climax**: Cui Ling slides the new memorial into the file and hands it up, opening the
  folded corner: "Corrected by the new reckoning — the reckoning comes from a memorial of
  mine, three years ago."
- **Choices**: plead guilty / let him submit it / turn on the superior
  - plead guilty: Pei Ji owns the alteration on the spot and keeps Cui Ling out of it — the law stands, he carries it.
  - let him submit it: Pei Ji says nothing and lets the new memorial go up — the method is new, the man is still sentenced.
  - turn on the superior: Pei Ji names who said "the memorial is sealed", dragging his superior in with him.
